Sampling is fixed in the engine (temp 0.8 / top-k 40 / top-p 0.95). “max new” applies to LLaMA-family models only.
Upload any image — it is downscaled to ≤16×16, tokenized (color-cube), and autoregressively reconstructed by a fresh tiny model that overfits this one input. Attention is O(pixels²) on CPU, so expect ~15–20 s. Toy-scale by design.
Upload a 16-bit PCM WAV — trimmed to ≤256 samples (≈16 ms @16 kHz), µ-law tokenized, and reconstructed. Attention is O(samples²) on CPU, so expect ~10 s. Toy-scale.
Upload a short video — frames are extracted (needs ffmpeg on PATH), embedded, and a tiny model is taught your caption then reproduces it. This is teach-then-reproduce: the multimodal front-end is untrained, so it proves the pipeline runs on your real frames end-to-end — not a real captioner. A frames preview (GIF) is shown.
